An application toolkit

Before you click “Apply”

01 / MATCH
Choose by research method

Psychology + experiments: PsychoPy, stats, surveys. Brain imaging: fMRI, EEG, TMS. Neuroengineering: Python, signals and ML. Molecular neuroscience: cell culture and imaging. Apply to methodological fit, not just the title.

02 / PREPARE
Keep a small application pack

A 1–2 page CV, short tailored research-interest paragraph, project/paper links, transcripts and, where asked, certificates and references. Check every advert: a generic email is rarely enough.

03 / VERIFY
Confirm the actual deadline

Official deadline and eligibility win over social posts or aggregators. Follow the official application method. Never pay unofficial recruiters for academic positions.

Useful search terms: “project associate cognitive neuroscience”, “JRF DST-CSRI psychology”, “research assistant fMRI India”, “EEG signal processing PhD”, “behavioural science RA”, “neuropsychology project staff”, “postdoctoral neuroscience India”. Also search faculty/lab pages — some positions appear there before an institutional recruitment index.
